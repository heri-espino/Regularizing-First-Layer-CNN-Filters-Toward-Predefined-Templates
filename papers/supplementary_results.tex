Under contrast ranking, $\Delta F_{sel}(4)=-0.022$ with 95\% interval $[-0.054,0.010]$, while $\Delta F_{rand}(4)=-0.083$ with interval $[-0.097,-0.068]$. The positive $\Delta U(4)=+0.061$ is therefore partly explained by the change in the random-channel baseline. At $k=8$, selected fidelity itself becomes slightly positive: $+0.006$ $[0.004,0.009]$. AUROC and validation-patch rankings show the same broad pattern.

For \texttt{two\_concepts}/TwoLayerCNN, selected fidelity at $k=4$ is negative under all three rankings (approximately -0.125, -0.121, and -0.087 for contrast, AUROC, and validation-patch, respectively). Validation-patch selected fidelity becomes positive at $k=8$ ($+0.0215$ with marginal interval $[0.0049,0.0381]$). These outcomes motivated a confirmatory hypothesis based on changes across patch sizes rather than on a fixed sign at one value of $k$.

\subsection{Stage-C validation-energy-matched control}\label{app:stage-c-energy}

A later post hoc control chose same-size alternative channel sets with validation replacement energy closest to the selected set. All 80 checkpoint evaluations completed. Matching quality depends strongly on patch size: singleton sets can have large relative mismatch, while $k=4$ and especially $k=8$ are generally tightly matched in the key TinyCNN setting. The selected-fidelity treatment contrast is unchanged by baseline construction and remains the direct quantity used to motivate Stage D. The energy-matched score is therefore reported as a secondary diagnostic.

\subsection{Transition to Stage D}\label{app:transition}

Stages A--C established that template retention preserves alignment and that prediction can improve after the retention penalty is released. They also showed that the release-minus-retention patching comparison changes with the number of patched channels. Because the original $U$ sign change combined selected and baseline fidelity, the Stage-D protocol was frozen around selected fidelity before new blocks were generated. Appendix~\ref{app:confirmation} reports that confirmation and the subsequent 1,200-model secondary sensitivity study.
